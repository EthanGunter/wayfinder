# Wayfinder

**Get your dreams out of your head and into reality.**

If you're opening other apps to stay on track, you're doing it wrong. Wayfinder is the one tool that connects your goals, breaks them into daily action, and keeps you accountable—powered by AI that actually understands your progress.

Stop bouncing between apps. Start becoming the person you want to be.

---

## What You Get

**Skill Sprints**  
Turn "I wish I could..." into a 6-month personalized plan. Tell Wayfinder your goal (learn guitar, ship a product, run a 5K), and it generates a roadmap. As you progress, it learns from your feedback and adapts the plan. No generic courses. Just what you actually need, right now.

**Projects**  
Break big ambitions into SMART tasks with built-in dependency logic. Wayfinder only suggests work you can *actually do today*—no more fake productivity on things that don't matter yet.

**Periodic Journals**  
Configurable journals that fit your rhythm. Daily timeblocking pulls from your active sprints and projects. Weekly/monthly reviews let you provide feedback to the system and course-correct. Set up the cadence that works for you—or skip what doesn't. The system adapts to how you work, not the other way around.

---

## The Philosophy

We built Wayfinder on decades of psychological research about motivation and habit. You shouldn't need to *understand* the science; you should just benefit from it. The tool is obsessively opinionated so you don't have to make a thousand decisions. Just decide what matters, and let the system handle the rest.
